Before you cook

Location: the US Consumer Product Safety Commission says to use charcoal grills outdoors only. Never use a gas or charcoal grill in a tent, camper, vehicle, garage or other enclosed space; carbon monoxide can kill. Keep the grill away from combustible materials and follow its manual's clearances.

Fire rules: check the campground's current restrictions before packing charcoal. Some parks restrict solid fuels while allowing shutoff-capable gas appliances; the rules vary by place and date.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Coleman include a propane cylinder?

No. Coleman lists the cylinder as sold separately for the documented tabletop version.

Can I use either grill inside a camper?

No. Use grills outdoors in a well-ventilated location, never inside an enclosed vehicle or shelter.

Is a flat-top griddle the same as a grill?

No. A flat cooking plate and an open grate handle grease and food differently. Choose the surface you intend to cook on.
