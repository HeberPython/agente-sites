Set up and verify the reference

  1. Read the exact model manual, inspect the housing and ensure the pendulum lock is in the correct position.
  2. Mount the tool on a stable surface within its stated self-leveling range. Magnetic mounts require suitable ferrous metal.
  3. Let a self-leveling beam settle and confirm that no out-of-level warning is flashing.
  4. Mark two reference points and recheck them before drilling, cutting, tiling or installing cabinets.
  5. For critical work, perform the manual's accuracy check and use an appropriate physical level or measurement as a second reference.

Laser radiation can injure eyes. Never stare into a beam, aim it at people, vehicles or reflective traffic surfaces, or let children use the tool unsupervised. A laser level is not eye protection and does not locate wiring or pipes behind a wall.

Frequently asked questions

Is a green beam always more accurate?

No. Beam color affects visibility, not accuracy by itself. Compare each exact model's stated accuracy and verify calibration according to its manual.

Can these lasers find studs or wires?

No. They project alignment references. Use an appropriate wall scanner and safe drilling practices before making holes.
