Use and storage checklist

  1. Find the official fastener specification and sequence for the exact vehicle or equipment.
  2. Choose a wrench whose calibrated range contains the target and install the correct undamaged socket.
  3. Set the unit and target, pull smoothly at the designated handle position and stop at the specified alert.
  4. Do not use a torque wrench as a breaker bar or exceed its range.
  5. Store it in its case, remove batteries for long storage if the manual directs and follow the calibration interval.

Wear eye protection and support vehicles correctly. Extensions, adapters, lubrication and joint condition can change the procedure; follow the service manual. A digital display does not compensate for an out-of-calibration tool or incorrect technique.

Frequently asked questions

Is the lowest displayed value always within the accuracy specification?

No. Manufacturers may limit stated accuracy to a percentage of the scale. Read the exact model documentation.

Can a torque wrench loosen stuck bolts?

It should not be used as a breaker bar unless its manufacturer explicitly permits that use. Use the correct loosening tool.
