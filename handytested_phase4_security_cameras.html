Frequently asked questions

Do all three store clips without a paid plan?

No. Ring's saved history needs a plan; Blink offers conditional local storage with compatible module and media; Arlo's compatible optional SmartHub may offer local storage, while cloud history needs a plan.

Is Blink Outdoor 4 rechargeable?

No. Its standard camera uses two non-rechargeable AA lithium batteries. Check the particular bundle and use the specified battery type.

Does 2K guarantee better identification?

No. Placement, lighting, motion, compression and distance all matter. We did not measure real-world identification performance.
