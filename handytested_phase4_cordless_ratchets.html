Use a power ratchet as a rundown tool

  1. Confirm drive size, socket condition and the vehicle or equipment service specification.
  2. Set direction before placing the socket on the fastener and keep loose clothing, hair and hands clear.
  3. Break a seized fastener with the appropriate hand tool or specified method rather than forcing the motor.
  4. Use the power ratchet to run the fastener, stopping before the joint is fully tightened.
  5. Finish critical fasteners with a calibrated torque wrench set to the manufacturer specification.

Disconnect the battery before clearing a jam or servicing the tool. Wear eye protection, support raised vehicles with rated stands and never rely on a jack alone. Check manuals for reactive-force warnings and do not use a damaged socket or battery.

Frequently asked questions

Can a cordless ratchet replace an impact wrench?

No. They overlap on some fasteners but have different speed, torque and reaction characteristics. Use the tool specified for the job.

Can I tighten lug nuts with one?

Do not rely on a cordless ratchet for final wheel torque. Follow the vehicle procedure and finish with a calibrated torque wrench.
