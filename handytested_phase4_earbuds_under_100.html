JLab Go Pop+: inexpensive, app-adjustable listening

The current Go Pop+ adds JLab app support and an integrated USB-C charging cable to a compact case. Buying advice

ANC versus awareness: ANC reduces some external noise using microphones and processing; Be Aware or ambient modes do the opposite. A good ear-tip seal matters for both comfort and passive isolation. Never use isolation so aggressively that you cannot hear traffic or other hazards.

Battery conditions: compare ANC-on hours where a maker publishes them. A case total usually includes multiple recharges and should not be read as continuous earbud runtime.

Phone and laptop use: JBL documents multipoint for the Beam 2. If two simultaneous devices are essential, verify that feature for the exact variant of any other pair rather than inferring it from Bluetooth version.
