Galaxy Buds4 Pro: Galaxy-specific audio features

Samsung documents two-way speakers, adaptive ANC and IP57 protection for the earbuds only. Its 24-bit/96 kHz mode requires select Galaxy hardware and software; connecting to an iPhone over Bluetooth does not unlock the same feature set. Samsung says the charging case is not IP57 rated.

Best for: a compatible Galaxy owner who will use its audio controls. Trade-off: the premium codec and some phone features are limited outside Samsung's ecosystem. Check the device compatibility chart before ordering.

Buying considerations

Fit first: ear-tip seal affects comfort and ANC. A manufacturer ANC superlative is not a substitute for a comparative test or a return policy that lets you confirm fit.

Water ratings: protection varies by model and between buds and case. None of these ratings makes a charging case suitable for intentional immersion; follow the manufacturer's drying guidance.

Real cost: compare replacement tips, warranty, return terms and current retailer pricing. The Amazon links here lead to exact-model searches, not verified direct product listings.

Frequently asked questions

Will Galaxy Buds4 Pro play audio on an iPhone?

Samsung says basic Bluetooth audio works, but Galaxy-only audio and device features do not all transfer.

Are the Bose second-generation buds waterproof?

No. Bose lists IPX4 water resistance, not waterproofing.

Does an eight-hour AirPods claim apply to every mode?

No. Apple's up-to figure is under stated ANC conditions; other features and volume can change playback.
