How to choose and what changed

Measure lens-to-screen distance and viewing size first. Compare native pixels rather than supported input, published ANSI/ISO brightness rather than unspecified marketing lumens, and check HDMI, speakers, streaming and replacement-lamp costs. Frequently asked questions

Does 4K support mean native 4K?

No. The Yaber Pro V9's manufacturer lists a native 1920 x 1080 panel while describing 4K input support.

Will the brightest projector give the sharpest film image?

No. PA503S has the highest listed white-output number here but only 800 x 600 native pixels. Source methods and room conditions also matter.

Do these three have the same streaming setup?

No. Confirm the app/device arrangement for the exact model and region. Epson requires an external HDMI streaming solution for movies; HDMI on its own is not an app platform.
