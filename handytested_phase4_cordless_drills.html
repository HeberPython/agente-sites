How to choose

  • Choose the package: a second battery, starter accessory set or V20 platform has different value depending on what you already own.
  • Match the bit and material: use a properly rated bit for wood, metal or masonry; these ordinary drill/drivers are not a substitute for a hammer drill in demanding masonry.
  • Use the clutch: start with a lower setting for screws, then adjust to reduce stripping or overdriving. Drill mode is for boring with an appropriate bit.
  • Compare batteries by exact label: maximum 20V naming does not specify usable runtime. Capacity, load, bit and material matter.

Safe setup

Check for hidden wiring and pipes before drilling, secure the workpiece, wear eye protection and keep hands clear of the rotating bit. Remove the battery before changing bits or making adjustments. Follow the exact manual for charging, storage, bit limits and any material-specific protection. A stud finder can help locate framing, but cannot certify that a wall cavity is clear of utilities.
