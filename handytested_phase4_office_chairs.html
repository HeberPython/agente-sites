Frequently asked questions

Can a chair cure back pain?

No such conclusion follows from manufacturer specifications. Adjust the whole workstation and seek professional advice for persistent symptoms.

Which seat depth should I choose?

One that supports your thighs while leaving space behind the knees and allowing your lower back to contact the backrest. OSHA recommends trying chairs where possible.

Are all HON Ignition 2.0 chairs alike?

No. Lumbar, arms, tilt, casters and seat-slider options vary by full configuration code.
