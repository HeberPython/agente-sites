What matters more than a headline ranking

  • Fit and return policy: pad pressure, headband shape, glasses and ear size are personal. A practical return window matters more than a universal comfort claim.
  • Wired fallback: verify cable and connector requirements for laptops, consoles or aircraft. ANC may need battery power even when audio is wired.
  • Multipoint: useful for a phone and laptop, but codec or app behavior can change when two sources are connected.
  • Microphones: manufacturer microphone counts do not prove call quality in your room. Test calls during the return period.
  • Hearing safety: ANC reduces selected ambient sounds; it is not certified hearing protection and should not replace situational awareness where hazards are present.

Frequently asked questions

Does ANC make a room completely silent?

No. Active noise cancellation can reduce some recurring background sound, but effectiveness changes with fit and noise type. Voices, alarms and sudden sounds can remain audible.

Can these headphones work with a cable when the battery is empty?

Sony documents passive wired operation. Bose says audio can continue passively when the battery is depleted, without ANC or Active EQ. Check soundcore's manual and your source connector before depending on wired fallback.
