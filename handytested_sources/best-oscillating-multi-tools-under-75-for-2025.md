## Editorial corrections

- Replaced Dremel MM35, misidentified BLACK+DECKER BDCMTJS and Rockwell RK5151K with three current, exact manufacturer-documented models.
- Removed invented weeks of cutting, sanding and scraping tests, blade-change timing, vibration and durability comparisons, star ratings and fixed prices.
- Removed the fixed under-$75 and 2025 promise. Prices and packages vary, particularly between a cordless bare tool and corded kits.
- Separated power source, included equipment, speed control and accessory interface instead of presenting unsupported performance rankings.
- Safety guidance follows manufacturer manuals: disconnect power before accessory changes, use an accessory rated for the material, inspect for hidden utilities and use suitable eye/hearing/dust protection.
- Amazon links remain exact-model tagged searches. No direct ASIN or listing was inferred.
