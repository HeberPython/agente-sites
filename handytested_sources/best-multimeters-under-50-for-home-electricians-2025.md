Auto-ranging vs. manual ranging is a significant usability factor: auto-ranging meters select measurement ranges automatically, while manual meters require you to know your expected values. For beginners, auto-ranging removes a common source of errors. Finally, consider the display count — a 6000-count display resolves smaller differences in readings than a 2000-count unit, giving you more precision when it counts.
