Buying and safety essentials

Abrasives: use discs matching the pad diameter and hole pattern. Progress through grits appropriate to the material and finish rather than assuming a higher OPM number prevents scratches.

Dust: a bag or port does not establish a measured capture rate. Follow the tool manual, use suitable respiratory and eye protection, and control dust at the source. The EPA warns that sanding paint in pre-1978 homes can release hazardous lead dust; get lead-safe guidance before disturbing old coatings.

Frequently asked questions

Is the DCW210B sold with a battery?

The cited DCW210B is tool only. Check a listing's contents, because a similarly named kit may include a battery and charger.

Does a dust bag make indoor sanding dust-free?

No. None of these manufacturer pages supports a dust-free claim. Choose an appropriate extraction setup and protective measures for the material.

Is BO5041K a different motor?

Makita identifies BO5041K as the case-included variant of BO5041. Verify the package contents of a specific offer.
