Cleaning and drying without overpromising

Vacuum loose hair first. Test cleaning solution on an inconspicuous area, follow both the surface-care label and machine instructions, and make extraction passes without oversaturating the carpet. Empty, rinse and dry tanks and attachments after use. EPA moisture guidance emphasizes drying carpet and backing promptly after water exposure; a seemingly dry surface can still hide damp backing. Keep pets away from treated, damp surfaces until they are fully dry and follow the cleaning formula's label. We cannot certify a formula or machine as universally pet-safe.

Frequently asked questions

Can a spot cleaner replace an upright?

It is practical for localized messes, but its small tool and tanks make room-scale carpet work a different task.

Does HydroSteam apply steam through the upholstery hose?

No. BISSELL says the 3432's included upholstery tools clean without steam. Check fabric-care codes before using any cleaning mode.

How soon is the carpet safe for pets?

There is no universal clock. Follow the formula label, ventilate and wait until the surface and backing are dry; manufacturer drying estimates are conditional.
