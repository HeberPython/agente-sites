Safety and ownership

Before mowing: inspect the lawn for stones and debris, keep people away, and never remove guards. Stop the blade and disconnect power or remove the battery before clearing a blockage or servicing. Follow the exact owner manual.

With a cord: CPSC advises an appropriate-gauge extension cord and a mowing pattern that keeps it away from the blade. Use an outdoor-rated cord and a protected outlet as directed by the mower manual. Do not mow wet grass.
