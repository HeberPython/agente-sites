Which is right for a pet home?

Roborock Q7 M5: mapping and an anti-tangle design

Roborock lists a main brush with a JawScrapers comb, an anti-tangle side brush, LiDAR navigation and three mopping water-flow levels. The Q7 M5 box contains a charging dock and mop module, not a self-emptying base. Its stated 10,000 Pa suction is a manufacturer measurement under specified Max+ conditions; it does not establish how much fur the robot will collect on your carpet.

Best for: a home that values mapped routes, anti-tangle hardware and optional light mopping. Trade-off: you empty the onboard bin yourself; mop cloth and brushes still require maintenance. Distinguish Q7 M5 from the Q7 M5+ dock bundle.

Buying considerations

Hair management: an anti-tangle design reduces maintenance claims, but no brush is maintenance-free. Check the brush and wheels regularly, especially with long-haired pets.

Navigation and obstacles: mapping does not guarantee recognition of every toy, cord or pet accident. Clear hazards and follow the manual's preparation guidance before running a robot unattended.

Total cost: compare replacement brushes, filters and any dock bags, not just a promotional robot price. Confirm whether a listing includes the self-empty base.

Does the Q7 M5 empty itself?
