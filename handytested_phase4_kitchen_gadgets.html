The six-quart pot and 120 V / 60 Hz specification are for the cited US model. It comes with a stainless-steel inner pot and steam rack, but the exact seller bundle should still be checked.

TempPro TP19H: check doneness with a probe

The TP19H is a folding instant-read thermometer with a rotating backlit display and motion-sensing wake. A thermometer reading is useful only when the probe is placed correctly and the target temperature is appropriate for the food. The USDA advises measuring the thickest portion and following its safe minimum internal temperature chart, rather than relying on color or texture alone.

Best for: regular checks during roasting, grilling and reheating. Trade-off: it is not a leave-in oven probe unless a manufacturer explicitly rates that use. Clean the probe as directed between foods, store the sharp tip safely and check calibration if readings seem inconsistent. Do not assume a marketing speed claim equals our own measurement.

Food safety and buying checks

For cooked food, use the USDA safe-temperature chart and measure in the thickest part. Clean and separate raw-food equipment. Chill perishables promptly even after vacuum sealing. Check voltage, counter footprint, seller bundle, replacement bags and the exact model before purchasing.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Duo 6QT also air fry?

No air-fry function is listed among the seven functions of the cited standard Duo. Duo Crisp is a different appliance with different parts.

Can TP19H stay in food while cooking?

Do not use it as a leave-in probe without explicit model-specific instructions. Insert it for a reading and follow the maker's operating limits.

Does vacuum sealing replace refrigeration?

No. Perishables still need appropriate refrigeration or freezing and safe handling.
