Coleman Triton two-burner: outdoor cooking

Coleman's Triton two-burner propane stove has two independently adjustable burners, wind guards, a removable grate and a manufacturer-rated 22,000 BTU total output. A propane cylinder is sold separately. Its official page showed unavailable at the time of research, so compare the exact Triton listing and seller rather than assuming stock; Triton+ is a different variant.

Check before buying: compatible propane, cookware fit, a stable outdoor surface and local flame restrictions. Practice setup before travel. Never run a fuel stove inside a tent, vehicle or other enclosed shelter: carbon monoxide can accumulate without a detectable smell.

Frequently asked questions

Can the stove be used in a tent with the door open?

No. Use a fuel-burning stove outdoors, away from enclosed shelters, and follow the manufacturer's instructions and campsite restrictions.

Does a four-person tent comfortably fit four with gear?

Not necessarily. Measure your pads and baggage against the listed floor area and account for sloping walls.

Does a 0F sleeping-bag label guarantee comfort at 0F?

No. The cited TETON model lists a substantially warmer comfort range; the pad and individual conditions matter too.
