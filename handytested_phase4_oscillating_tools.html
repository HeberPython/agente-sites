Choose the blade before the tool setting

  1. Identify the material and task: wood, metal, grout, scraping and sanding need different accessories.
  2. Confirm that the accessory mount or supplied adaptor fits the exact tool.
  3. Disconnect the plug or remove the battery before installing or adjusting an accessory.
  4. Inspect the work area for hidden wiring, plumbing, fasteners and other hazards before cutting.
  5. Start with the manufacturer's recommended speed and technique rather than forcing a dull or unsuitable blade.

Wear suitable eye protection and use hearing and dust protection for the task. Secure the workpiece, keep hands and cords away from the accessory, and allow hot blades to cool before handling. Follow the exact tool and accessory manuals; an oscillating tool does not make an unknown wall cavity safe to cut.

Frequently asked questions

Can one oscillating blade cut wood and metal?

Only when the blade manufacturer rates it for both. A wood-only blade can be damaged by nails or metal. Match the blade to the material and replace worn accessories.

Is a cordless bare tool cheaper than a corded kit?

Not necessarily. Add the cost of a compatible battery and charger if you do not already own them, then compare the actual package and current price.
