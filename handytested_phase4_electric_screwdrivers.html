Match bit, pilot hole and torque to the fastener

  1. Choose the exact bit profile and size; a loose fit damages both bit and screw.
  2. Check for hidden wiring or plumbing and use a pilot hole when the material and fastener call for one.
  3. Seat the bit squarely, start at low force and stop when the fastener is flush.
  4. Finish delicate or precision hardware by hand when needed.
  5. Remove the bit and disconnect charging power before cleaning or inspecting the tool.

Wear eye protection when drilling or where chips can be produced. Do not drive into an energized fixture, use damaged bits or charge with an incompatible supply. Follow the furniture, anchor and fastener instructions as well as the tool manual.

Frequently asked questions

Can a 4V screwdriver drill holes?

Some packages include a small drill bit, but these tools are primarily screwdrivers. Use a drill/driver for larger holes or demanding material.

Does a circuit sensor prove power is off?

No. Detection can be affected by distance, construction and procedure. De-energize and verify according to accepted electrical-safety practices.
