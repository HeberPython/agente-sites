SKIL DP9505-00: straightforward 10-inch drilling

SKIL lists a 6.2-amp induction motor, five belt-selected speeds from 610 to 2,800 RPM, a 1/2-inch keyed chuck, a laser, a work light and a table that tilts 45 degrees left or right. Buying and safety essentials

Capacity: match swing, spindle travel, chuck and table space to your workpiece. A laser is an alignment aid, not proof of drilling accuracy. Verify the bit, material and recommended speed in the machine's manual.

Secure the setup: fasten the press as instructed and clamp the workpiece instead of holding it by hand. The W1667 manual warns that a loose workpiece can spin or be thrown. It also calls for eye protection, no loose clothing or gloves near rotating parts, and disconnecting power before setup or service. Follow the manual for your exact machine, not a short buying guide.

Frequently asked questions

Does a 10-inch swing mean a 10-inch-deep hole?

No. Swing describes reach across the table; spindle travel and setup determine how deep a single feed can go.

Is a laser necessary?

No. It can help position a mark, but proper bit choice, secure workholding and setup matter more.

Are these guaranteed under $300 on Amazon?

No. The category was supported by US manufacturer or official-seller listings at research time. Current Amazon offers can differ; confirm seller, exact model and final price.

Can the W1667 sand as well as drill?

Yes, when fitted and operated as its manual specifies with the appropriate sanding accessories. It does not have the reach of larger press models.
