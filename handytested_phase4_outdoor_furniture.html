Planning and safe assembly

Measure the site and doorways, read the full parts and board list, budget for outdoor-suitable lumber/finish and check the surface is level. Use eye protection and appropriate tools for cutting; secure boards while working. Recheck fasteners and stability before use, follow the maker's weight limits and get help lifting larger pieces. Keep cushions and stored items protected according to their own care guidance.
