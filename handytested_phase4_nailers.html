Safety and the old list

OSHA warns that nail guns can cause serious injury and that contact/multi-shot actuation presents more risk than single-shot sequential actuation. Read the specific tool manual, wear eye protection, keep your free hand clear of the exit path and check behind thin material before driving. Remove the battery before clearing a jam, changing fasteners or servicing. A dry-fire lockout or nose contact does not make the tool harmless.

Frequently asked questions

Are brad nails suitable for structural framing?

No. These 18-gauge tools are intended for light trim and similar fastening, not framing connections requiring specified structural fasteners.

Does tool-only include a battery?

Not for the listed DCN680B or P321. The CMCN618C1 is a kit; always check the actual offer.

Which firing mode should a beginner use?

Follow the manual and prefer deliberate sequential actuation where available. OSHA identifies greater injury risk with multi-shot contact triggering.
