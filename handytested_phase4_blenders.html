Buying and care

Batch size: compare usable liquid fill lines, not marketing pitcher volume. For thick ingredients, follow the maker's liquid and ingredient-loading guidance rather than forcing the blades.

Cleaning: unplug before reaching near blades. Follow the manual for which parts are dishwasher-safe; do not immerse a motor base. Inspect the blade assembly and container for damage before use.
