What each model offers

Klein Tools MM325: simpler functions, manual ranges

Klein lists the MM325 as a manual-ranging meter for up to 600 V AC/DC, 10 A DC current, resistance, continuity, diode and battery checks. Its product page states CAT III 600 V and includes test leads and batteries. Lead-alert LEDs help show lead placement, but they do not replace checking the dial, jacks and lead ratings yourself.

Best for: users who understand manual range selection and need a limited function set. Skip if: you need AC current measurement, auto-ranging or documented True RMS.

Klein Tools MM420: auto-ranging with more functions

The MM420 is the manufacturer's named replacement for the discontinued MM400. Klein documents CAT III 600 V, True RMS, auto-ranging, AC/DC voltage and current, plus temperature, capacitance, frequency, duty cycle, diode and continuity functions. The box includes a thermocouple and test leads with safety caps. Its lead-alert feature is a reminder, not a guarantee against a wrong setup.

Best for: buyers who need those extra measurements and prefer auto-ranging. Trade-off: the greater number of modes creates more setup choices; review the manual and fuse requirements before measuring current.

Fluke 110: focused voltage troubleshooting

Fluke's US page identifies the 110 as a True RMS meter for AC voltage, resistance and continuity with a backlit display. It specifies CAT III 600 V and a 600 V maximum between any terminal and earth ground. It does not list a current-measurement function, so choose a different model if that is essential.

Best for: users who want a focused voltage and continuity tool. Skip if: you need current, temperature or capacitance measurement from this meter.

Safety and buying checks

A CAT rating is tied to an electrical environment and voltage limit, not a general safety score. Check the markings on both meter and leads, inspect them before use, and follow the model's manual. Fluke's measurement-category guide explains why the category must match the location. Klein warns against resistance or continuity checks on energized circuits and instructs users to verify meter operation on a known source. Do not open a service panel or probe live mains without appropriate training and procedures.

Frequently asked questions

Does CAT III 600 V make a meter safe for any home job?

No. The actual circuit, voltage, lead assembly, meter condition and user's procedures all matter. A category marking does not confer electrical training.

When does True RMS matter?

It matters when AC waveforms are distorted rather than clean sine waves. Verify the exact functions marked True RMS; the Fluke 110 page specifically describes True RMS AC voltage.
