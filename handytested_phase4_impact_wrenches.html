Safe use and total cost

Choose an impact-rated socket of the correct size, wear eye protection and follow the tool manual. Start threads by hand to avoid cross-threading. For wheel hardware, follow the vehicle's specified sequence and torque; finish with a hand torque wrench, not the impact gun's setting.
